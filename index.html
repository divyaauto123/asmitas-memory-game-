A Hidden Letter

My dearest Asmita,

There are some words I keep folded inside my heart, the way we keep the deepest diyas lit inside the temple of our home. Tonight, under this Diwali sky, I want to unfold them for you.

Before you, my life was like a night waiting for its festival. You arrived, and suddenly every corner was lit — not with lamps, but with your laughter, your patience, your quiet strength, and the way you love without conditions.

You are the reason our home feels like a celebration even on ordinary days. You are the warmth in my mornings and the peace in my nights. You are the diya I never want to see go dim.

Thank you for choosing me, every single day. Thank you for being my best friend, my confidant, my home. I promise to keep choosing you — in this Diwali, and in every Diwali the future brings.

May the light of our love only grow brighter with time.

Forever yours,
Manoj ❤️
